Click anywhere to play

Press B to open your Backpack — it starts already packed with the real Scout's 10 Essentials plus a tent, sleeping bag, sleeping pad and rope. Pitch your tent (a real shelter you can walk into) and take a bearing with your compass to earn merit badges right away. Chop a tree for wood and leaves, put down your Camp Workbench, and press E beside it to craft — twist leaves into rope, then set up a campfire, a lantern and your troop flag — each one earns a merit badge too, and badges promote you from Scout all the way to Eagle Scout. Press M to see your sash, K near your tent after dark to sleep through the night, or H any time for the full hotkey list. Rabbits and deer are harmless — the moose fights back if provoked, and the black bear attacks on sight. Progress saves automatically in this browser.
